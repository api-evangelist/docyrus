## WebMCP Tools

- [WebMCP: Platform, Catalog, Pricing & Contact Tools](https://docyrus.com/): document.modelContext is the standard Chrome WebMCP API available on every docyrus.com page. Call getTools() to discover tools, then executeTool(tool, JSON.stringify(args)) to invoke them. Docyrus is an AI Native application development and publishing platform, not a CRM or single-purpose SaaS product. Sales CRM and similar apps are examples built on the platform. Available tools: get_platform_overview (no args), list_apps (query?, category?), get_app_details (slug), list_agents (query?, category?, gallery_group?), get_agent_details (slug), list_pricing_plans (no args), estimate_docyrus_cost (runs, ai_credits, plan_id?, support_id?, users?), recommend_plan (runs, ai_credits, users; all required), submit_sales_inquiry (firstname, lastname, email, phone, company_website, how_can_we_help_you). Pricing note: Docyrus does NOT charge per seat; user count is a plan-eligibility check only, not a cost multiplier. Starter plan is free for the first 6 months when billed annually only; monthly billing starts at $36/month from day one.
